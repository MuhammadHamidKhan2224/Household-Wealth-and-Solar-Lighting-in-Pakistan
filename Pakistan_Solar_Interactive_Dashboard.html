How to read this investigation

Percentages describe households using the supplied sampling weights. Confidence intervals approximate clustering by resampling PSUs or using PSU-cluster score covariance. The workbook lacks the original survey strata, finite population corrections and official replicate weights. Complete-case exclusions were followed by retaining the original weights.

Solar outcomes describe lighting. They do not measure electricity generation, capacity, CO₂ savings, grid reliability, or verified grid access. Monetary asset and cash measures have partial coverage. This single-round cross-section supports adjusted associations, not causal effects.

Technical reference for concentration curves and binary Erreygers indices: WHO technical notes. Data definitions: supplied workbook’s Guide sheet. Full methods, model tables and metadata are included in the results ZIP.
